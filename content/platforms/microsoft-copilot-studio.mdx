Microsoft Copilot Studio represents the convergence of Power Virtual Agents and Azure AI capabilities, offering a low-code environment for building enterprise copilots. The platform's deep integration with Microsoft 365 ecosystem—SharePoint, Teams, Dynamics 365—makes it a natural choice for organizations already standardized on Microsoft technologies.

The Power Platform foundation provides visual workflow design, pre-built connectors to hundreds of services, and enterprise-grade governance through Entra ID. Azure AI Foundry extends these capabilities with access to advanced AI models, custom model deployment, and hybrid RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) patterns using your organization's data.

Ideal for Microsoft-centric enterprises and citizen developer programs. The low-code approach accelerates time-to-value for business teams while IT retains control through centralized governance. Licensing combines per-user Copilot Studio fees with consumption-based Azure AI charges, making it predictable for budgeting. Best suited for organizations seeking to empower business users to build copilots while maintaining enterprise security and compliance standards.
