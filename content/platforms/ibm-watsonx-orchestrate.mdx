At Think 2026 (May 5), IBM announced the next-generation watsonx Orchestrate—an agentic control plane for the multi-agent era (private preview). The new platform supports IBM native agents, Langflow, LangGraph, and A2A protocol agents, with built-in observability, tracing, and runtime evaluation. An eCommerce pilot allows customers to purchase agents directly from IBM's product site. IBM watsonx Orchestrate brings together IBM's decades of enterprise AI experience with modern foundation models. The platform enables organizations to orchestrate AI-powered automation through a skills-based approach—pre-built skills handle common enterprise tasks while custom skills extend functionality for specific business processes.

Version 2.7.0 introduces Workspaces CLI management, Skill specifications for standardized automation definitions, Genesys platform support for contact center deployments, voice integration via Deepgram and ElevenLabs, and MCP tool support for standardized agent-tool connectivity. The platform supports multiple foundation models including IBM Granite, Meta Llama, and models from other providers, allowing organizations to select the right model for each task. Deep integration with IBM Cloud Paks means agents can leverage existing investments in automation, data, and security. The conversational builder enables business users to create automation workflows through natural language.

Best suited for IBM-centric enterprises or those with complex hybrid cloud environments. The platform's governance capabilities—including model lineage, bias detection, and explainability—address regulatory requirements in financial services, healthcare, and government. Strong where enterprises need to modernize existing automation investments while maintaining enterprise-grade controls.
