IBM watsonx Orchestrate brings together IBM's decades of enterprise AI experience with modern foundation models. The platform enables organizations to orchestrate AI-powered automation through a skills-based approach—pre-built skills handle common enterprise tasks while custom skills extend functionality for specific business processes.

The platform supports multiple foundation models including IBM Granite, Meta Llama, and models from other providers, allowing organizations to select the right model for each task. Deep integration with IBM Cloud Paks means agents can leverage existing investments in automation, data, and security. The conversational builder enables business users to create automation workflows through natural language.

Best suited for IBM-centric enterprises or those with complex hybrid cloud environments. The platform's governance capabilities—including model lineage, bias detection, and explainability—address regulatory requirements in financial services, healthcare, and government. Strong where enterprises need to modernize existing automation investments while maintaining enterprise-grade controls.
