Zapier is the most widely adopted automation platform with 7,000+ app integrations, now expanding into AI agent territory with Zapier Central. The AI layer lets users describe automations in natural language, and the platform generates the multi-step Zap workflow — dramatically reducing the time from idea to working automation.

Central, Zapier's AI orchestration product, enables agents that can reason across your connected apps, make decisions based on data from multiple sources, and execute multi-step workflows autonomously. Combined with Tables (Zapier's built-in database), agents can maintain state, track progress, and reference structured data without external infrastructure. The 7,000+ integration library means agents can interact with virtually any SaaS tool without custom API work.

Zapier is best suited for teams that need broad, shallow automation across many tools rather than deep integration with a few. It excels at connecting marketing, sales, support, and operations tools into automated workflows. The pricing is accessible ($49-149/month) compared to enterprise iPaaS platforms, making it practical for small-to-mid teams. The tradeoff is sophistication: complex data transformations, custom business logic, and high-volume enterprise scenarios (100K+ tasks/month) are better served by Workato, Tray.ai, or developer-first platforms.
