SAP Joule is deeply embedded across SAP's cloud portfolio, providing AI assistance that understands SAP's complex business processes and data models. Unlike general-purpose AI, Joule knows the difference between a purchase order and a sales order, understands organizational hierarchies, and respects SAP's authorization model.

The copilot can execute transactions, generate reports, and provide insights across S/4HANA, SuccessFactors, Ariba, Concur, and other SAP applications. Natural language queries translate to complex SAP operations—users can ask "show my team's open purchase requisitions" and Joule navigates the underlying complexity. Role-based personalization ensures each user sees relevant capabilities. With 2,500+ Joule Skills and 30+ specialized agents, the platform covers an expanding range of business processes out of the box. Joule Studio, now GA, lets teams build custom agents, and the agent-to-agent protocol enables cross-system communication between Joule agents. A Google Cloud partnership announced in April 2026 further extends Joule's infrastructure and model options. Joule is available in 13 languages.

Best suited for SAP-centric enterprises looking to improve user productivity and reduce SAP training requirements. Joule's business context awareness means it understands not just what data exists, but what it means in business terms. Strong in manufacturing, retail, and professional services where SAP is the ERP backbone and where reducing SAP complexity is a strategic priority.
