SAP Joule is deeply embedded across SAP's cloud portfolio, providing AI assistance that understands SAP's complex business processes and data models. Unlike general-purpose AI, Joule knows the difference between a purchase order and a sales order, understands organizational hierarchies, and respects SAP's authorization model.

At **Sapphire 2026** (May 11-13), SAP announced **Joule Studio 2.0** — a fully managed, zero-config version with free design-time access through end of 2026. SAP also invested approximately **$60M in n8n** (May 12), with plans to embed the n8n workflow canvas directly in Joule Studio by Q3 2026, enabling visual workflow orchestration alongside agent building. A **Vercel partnership** adds modern UI experiences to Joule-powered applications. First production customers are onboarding in June 2026, with a desktop app and bidirectional A2A protocol planned for Q4 2026.

The copilot can execute transactions, generate reports, and provide insights across S/4HANA, SuccessFactors, Ariba, Concur, and other SAP applications. Natural language queries translate to complex SAP operations—users can ask "show my team's open purchase requisitions" and Joule navigates the underlying complexity. Role-based personalization ensures each user sees relevant capabilities. With 2,500+ Joule Skills and 30+ specialized agents, the platform covers an expanding range of business processes out of the box. Joule is available in 13 languages.

Best suited for SAP-centric enterprises looking to improve user productivity and reduce SAP training requirements. Joule's business context awareness means it understands not just what data exists, but what it means in business terms. Strong in manufacturing, retail, and professional services where SAP is the ERP backbone and where reducing SAP complexity is a strategic priority.
