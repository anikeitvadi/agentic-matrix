ServiceNow AI Agents combines the platform's deep enterprise service management expertise with generative AI through Now Assist. Agents can resolve IT incidents autonomously, route requests intelligently, and generate knowledge articles—all while maintaining the audit trails and approval workflows enterprises require.

As of April 2026, ServiceNow offers three distinct AI pricing tiers: **Foundation** (generative AI tasks like summarization and content generation), **Advanced** (deterministic workflows combined with AI agent capabilities), and **Prime** (full role replacement with autonomous agents that can handle end-to-end processes). The Context Engine, now embedded across all products, provides agents with deep understanding of organizational data, relationships, and business context.

The platform's strength is its unified data model: agents understand relationships between users, assets, incidents, and changes through the CMDB. This contextual awareness enables intelligent automation that considers impact and dependencies. Virtual Agent handles conversational interactions across channels while Now Assist provides generative capabilities for content creation and summarization.

Best suited for organizations using ServiceNow for ITSM looking to increase automation and self-service. The platform extends beyond IT to HR Service Delivery, Customer Service Management, and Security Operations. Strong in large enterprises where ServiceNow is the enterprise service backbone and where audit trails and change management are critical requirements.
