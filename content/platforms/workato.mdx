Workato is the enterprise iPaaS leader with the largest pre-built connector library in the market (1,200+), now expanding into AI-powered automation with recipe generation and intelligent workflow orchestration. Its strength is connecting complex enterprise systems — ERP, CRM, HRIS, finance — with minimal custom code.

The AI recipe builder lets users describe what they want to automate in natural language, and Workato generates the integration logic. This is particularly valuable for IT teams managing hundreds of cross-system workflows who want to add AI capabilities without rebuilding their integration layer. The Workbot feature brings automation directly into Slack and Teams, enabling conversational agent interactions that trigger enterprise workflows.

Workato is best suited for organizations already invested in enterprise SaaS (Salesforce, SAP, Workday, NetSuite) who need agent capabilities layered onto existing integrations. The platform excels at structured, repeatable workflows with clear business rules. For unstructured reasoning or complex multi-step agent behaviors, developer-first frameworks offer more flexibility. Pricing is enterprise-tier — expect $25K-$60K+ annually depending on recipe volume and connector requirements.
