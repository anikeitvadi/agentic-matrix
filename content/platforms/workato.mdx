Workato is the enterprise iPaaS leader with the largest pre-built connector library in the market (1,200+), now expanding into AI-powered automation with recipe generation and intelligent workflow orchestration. Its strength is connecting complex enterprise systems — ERP, CRM, HRIS, finance — with minimal custom code.

The new "Workato One" edition introduces Agentic Orchestration, enabling AI agents that can autonomously manage complex multi-system workflows. Workflows can now be **exposed as Remote MCP Servers**, allowing any MCP-compatible AI agent to discover and invoke Workato automations — a significant step toward interoperability in the agentic ecosystem. Recipe IQ uses AI for smarter flow construction, helping users build more efficient recipes. The Workbot feature brings automation directly into Slack and Teams, enabling conversational agent interactions that trigger enterprise workflows.

Workato is best suited for organizations already invested in enterprise SaaS (Salesforce, SAP, Workday, NetSuite) who need agent capabilities layered onto existing integrations. The platform excels at structured, repeatable workflows with clear business rules. For unstructured reasoning or complex multi-step agent behaviors, developer-first frameworks offer more flexibility. Pricing is custom/sales-led, typically $40K-$250K/year depending on recipe volume and connector requirements.
