UiPath is the dominant RPA platform, now integrating generative AI to evolve from rule-based automation into intelligent agent behavior. The key differentiator is combining screen-level automation (clicking buttons, filling forms, moving data between legacy systems) with AI reasoning — something pure LLM-based agent platforms cannot do.

Document Understanding uses AI to extract structured data from invoices, contracts, and forms with high accuracy, feeding directly into RPA workflows. Process mining analyzes existing business processes to identify automation opportunities, making UiPath particularly strong for organizations that want to systematically automate across departments rather than build one-off agents.

UiPath is ideal for organizations with significant manual, repetitive processes across legacy systems that lack APIs — think healthcare claims processing, insurance underwriting, financial reconciliation. If your bottleneck is human data entry between systems, UiPath's RPA+AI approach is often more practical than building API-first agents. The limitation is flexibility: custom reasoning, multi-turn conversations, and unstructured problem-solving are better handled by LLM-native platforms.
