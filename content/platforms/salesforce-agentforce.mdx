Salesforce Agentforce represents the evolution of Einstein AI into autonomous agents that can take action across the entire Salesforce ecosystem. Agents have native access to Customer 360 data, meaning they understand customer context without complex integration work. The Trust Layer provides enterprise governance including audit trails, data masking, and toxicity detection.

As of mid-2026, Agentforce supports **multi-agent orchestration**: agents work together as a unified team, coordinating across sales, service, and commerce processes. Agents are now usable directly in **Flow Builder**, making it easy to embed autonomous AI steps into existing automation flows. The new **Customer Engagement Agent** provides 24/7 buyer qualification, and **Tableau MCP** enables data-expert agents that can analyze and surface insights from Tableau datasets. Note that **Agentforce Vibes** transitioned to paid access on June 1, 2026, requiring Flex Credits or a paid license.

Pre-built agent templates accelerate deployment for common use cases: Sales agents qualify leads and update opportunities, Service agents resolve cases and escalate appropriately, Commerce agents assist with product recommendations and order management. Custom agents can be built using low-code tools or Apex for complex scenarios.

Best suited for Salesforce-centric organizations looking to automate customer-facing processes. The per-conversation pricing model aligns costs with value delivered. Industry clouds (Financial Services, Healthcare, Manufacturing) include specialized agents with domain-specific knowledge and compliance guardrails. Strong in B2B sales, customer service, and commerce where Salesforce is the system of record.
