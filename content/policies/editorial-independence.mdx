## Our Commitment

Agentic Decisions exists to provide vendor-neutral guidance for enterprise IT leaders evaluating AI agent platforms. We accept no platform sponsorships, affiliate payments, or vendor compensation of any kind. Our recommendations are based solely on your requirements and our honest assessment of each platform's capabilities.

This commitment to independence isn't just a policy — it's the foundation of our value proposition. If you can't trust our analysis, we've failed our mission.

## How We Evaluate Platforms

Our platform assessments follow a structured methodology:

**Structured Criteria**: We evaluate every platform across consistent dimensions including core capabilities, pricing transparency, documentation quality, integration options, and enterprise readiness. No platform gets special treatment or custom criteria.

**Regular Re-Verification**: Each platform entry includes a `lastVerified` timestamp showing when we last confirmed the information. We re-verify platform details quarterly or when major updates are announced. Stale data serves no one — if we can't keep information current, we mark it as outdated or remove it.

**Practitioner Perspective**: Our assessments are informed by real-world integration experience. The author spent years implementing enterprise integrations and knows the gap between vendor marketing and production reality. We focus on the questions IT leaders actually need answered: What can this really do? How transparent is the pricing? Can I trust this in production?

**Evidence-Based Analysis**: Every capability claim is verified against official documentation, pricing pages, and public information. We link to sources so you can verify our work. If a platform's documentation is unclear or pricing is hidden, we say so — that's data too.

## What We Don't Do

To maintain independence, we explicitly refuse certain practices common in platform recommendation sites:

**No Paid Reviews**: We do not accept payment for favorable reviews, sponsored content, or promoted placements. Platforms cannot buy their way to better ratings or higher visibility.

**No Vendor Preview**: Platforms do not get to preview, approve, or influence their assessments before publication. We may reach out to clarify technical questions, but editorial control stays with us.

**No Affiliate Relationships**: We do not participate in affiliate programs, referral fees, or revenue sharing with platforms. We make no money when you choose a platform — our only incentive is to give you accurate guidance.

**No Sponsored Recommendations**: The assessment engine recommends platforms based purely on match quality between your requirements and platform capabilities. No platform can pay for preferential recommendation.

## Conflicts of Interest

**Current Status**: As of February 2026, we have no vendor relationships, sponsorships, partnerships, or financial arrangements with any AI agent platform provider.

**Future Policy**: Should we ever establish partnerships (for example, to access beta features for early evaluation), we will disclose these relationships clearly and conspicuously on affected content. Any partnership will be structured to preserve editorial independence.

**User Data**: Assessment responses you provide are used only to generate your personalized recommendations and to improve the matching algorithm. We do not sell, share, or provide assessment data to vendors. Your requirements are your business.

**Author Background**: The founder has professional experience implementing enterprise integrations but holds no equity positions in, and receives no compensation from, any AI agent platform vendor. This site was built to solve a real problem we observed: IT leaders need honest guidance, not vendor pitches.

## Corrections and Concerns

We're committed to accuracy, but we're not perfect. If you find incorrect information, outdated data, or potential bias in our assessments:

**Report Concerns**: Email corrections@agenticdecisions.com with specific details. We investigate every report and update content when errors are confirmed.

**Suggest Updates**: Platform capabilities evolve quickly. If you notice a platform has added features we haven't captured, or if our `lastVerified` timestamp is more than 6 months old, let us know.

**Challenge Bias**: If you believe we've shown bias toward or against a platform, we want to hear about it. Provide specific examples and we'll review our analysis.

We typically respond to correction requests within 48 hours and update content within one week of confirming an error.
