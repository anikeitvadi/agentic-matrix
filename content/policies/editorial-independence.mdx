## Our Commitment

Agentic Decisions provides vendor-neutral guidance for teams evaluating AI agent platforms. We accept no sponsorships, affiliate payments, or vendor compensation. Recommendations are based solely on your requirements and a deterministic scoring algorithm applied equally to every platform.

## How We Evaluate Platforms

**Structured Capabilities**: Every platform is scored against the same criteria — integration support, compliance certifications, use case strengths, budget fit, and stack compatibility. No keyword matching or subjective ratings. Scoring uses structured capability flags defined in each platform's data file.

**Real Pricing Data**: Cost estimates use verified pricing from official documentation and pricing pages. Token rates, subscription tiers, and per-conversation costs are captured as structured data and fed into a PERT-based cost calculator. We do not use tier-based proxies or rough estimates.

**Pricing Disclaimer**: Platform pricing changes frequently. While we verify pricing data against official sources and note the last verification date on every platform page, costs may have changed since our last review. Always confirm current pricing directly with the vendor before making procurement decisions.

**Regular Verification**: Each platform entry includes a `lastVerified` date. We re-verify platform details when major updates are announced. If information cannot be confirmed, we mark it as such.

**Deterministic Scoring**: The recommendation engine uses Simple Additive Weighting (SAW) with dynamic weight derivation based on your assessment inputs. Every score is auditable — the results page shows the exact calculation for each criterion. AI is used only as an optional explanation layer on the results page; it does not influence the ranking.

## What We Don't Do

**No Paid Placements**: Platforms cannot buy higher rankings, better ratings, or promoted positions. The scoring algorithm treats every platform identically.

**No Affiliate Revenue**: We do not participate in referral programs or revenue sharing. We make no money when you choose a platform.

**No Vendor Preview**: Platforms do not preview or approve their assessments before publication.

## Data Handling

**Assessment Data**: Your assessment responses are processed client-side to generate recommendations. Form data is stored in your browser's localStorage and is not sent to any server, except when you explicitly choose to generate an optional AI decision brief — in which case selected assessment fields and top recommendation data are sent to OpenAI's API for analysis.

**No Tracking**: We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party data collection.

## Corrections

If you find incorrect information or outdated data, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository. We investigate every report and update content when errors are confirmed.
